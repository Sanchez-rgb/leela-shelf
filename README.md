## Who it's for

Elementary general music teachers, substitutes who get handed a music class with no plan, and teachers who would rather not rebuild the same file every August.

## Delivery and refunds

Instant download after PayPal checkout, plus a backup copy by email within a few hours if anything goes wrong. Reply to your receipt email within 14 days for a refund. No form.

## An honest note

The files were drafted with AI assistance and edited for classroom practicality. Every pack includes the editable version, so if something does not fit your room, you change it. These are ready-to-run starting points, not a curriculum.
